Streličarski Klub "Rijeka" is also commonly known as "SK Rijeka". The abbreviation "SK" in the domain stands for "Streličarski klub". Therefore, sk-rijeka.hr directly represents Streličarski Klub "Rijeka" and is the nonprofit organization's official and primary domain.

The following public page explicitly confirms the relationship between the organization's full name, abbreviated name and primary domain:
https://sk-rijeka.hr/sluzbena-domena.html

The domain is used for the club's official website, organizational communication, Google Workspace and other digital services.
