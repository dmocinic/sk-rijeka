Official domain confirmation

sk-rijeka.hr is the official and primary domain of Streličarski Klub “Rijeka” (Archery Club Rijeka). The club is also commonly known as SK Rijeka. The abbreviation “SK” stands for “Streličarski klub”, which directly explains and connects the domain name sk-rijeka.hr with the nonprofit organization. The domain is used for the club’s official website, organizational communication and digital services.

Dok je puna web-stranica kluba u završnoj pripremi, ova javna stranica služi kao službena potvrda identiteta i primarne domene organizacije.
